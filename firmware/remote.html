Tap Start when you get on. Your train turns purple on the map, and your name and arrival time go on the sign. Change trains by choosing the new one and tapping Start again.

A korok pops up on the sign for a few seconds, then the sign goes back to what it was showing.

Line up the sign

Lights the sign's outermost ring of LEDs white and the next ring yellow. When the sign sits square behind the bezel, an even half-row of white shows on all four sides; where it's off, you see all of the white or some yellow.
