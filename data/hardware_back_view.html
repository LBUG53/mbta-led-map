MBTA map: hardware on the back of the panel

Seen from behind, so left and right are swapped compared with the map. The sign is at the lower left here.

  • Tiles: three printed black tiles, coloured here by tile. Each double collar holds one station's two pixels.
  • Joints A to D (red dashed rings): a solid knob on one tile drops into a C-shaped socket on the next. A and B join the lower-left tile to the upper-left; C and D join the upper-left to the upper-right.
  • Locating collars (black ring and cross): two per tile. They match the ringed reference holes on the drill guide.
  • Glue tabs (white discs with a dot): 28 in all.
  • Yellow blocks: zip-tie anchors. Below the Matrix Portal they hold the two lever nuts (+5 V and GND) and the barrel-jack adapter the power supply plugs into; the one against the frame holds the supply cable where it comes in.
  • Zip ties (orange): one over the Matrix Portal, one over the Sparkle Motion, one on each of the four anchors: 6, plus any used to bundle wire.
  • Wires: grey band = ribbon cable from the Matrix Portal to the sign; red and black = power to the sign; red dashes = 5 V and ground to the Sparkle Motion; blue = the Matrix Portal's USB-C lead, from the lever nuts. The supply cable and the Sparkle Motion's pigtail share the narrow gap between the Sparkle Motion and the sign.
  • Green dots 1, 2, 3: where each output's chain of pixels begins. The dotted green lines are the leads from the Sparkle Motion; route them between collars and hold them with the printed wire clips.
  • Cups: the five always-dark spacer pixels park in these, next to the hop they hang in.
  • Proposed, not final: the positions of the two boards, lever nuts, jack adapter and cups (the Sparkle Motion is in the bottom lane so its three leads are short). The tiles, joints, tabs and locating collars are exact.
